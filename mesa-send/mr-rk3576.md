Title: rocket: RK3576 NPU enablement, regular convolution

---

This is the minimal series to get one convolution running on the RK3576 NPU,
in the shape Tomeu asked for on the linux-media thread. Five patches, 648 lines,
no debug environment variables.

What it covers

A regular convolution over uint8 per tensor operands, one task per operation.
The RK3576 register map is not the RK3588 one at the same offsets, so it gets
its own encoder rather than conditionals threaded through the existing one, and
its per channel requantisation lives in 64 byte records rather than a flat array
of biases.

What it declines

A depthwise convolution, a pointwise one, and one whose input is an image with
up to four channels each take a different datapath and a different weight
layout, and none of them is implemented here. rkt_ml_operation_supported turns
them down so they run on the CPU instead of being encoded as if they were
regular convolutions. Before that guard existed, MobileNet's 224x224x3 first
layer went down the regular path with a weight buffer in the wrong layout and
returned a plausible looking wrong answer.

Verified on a Radxa ROCK 4D

  conv2d-cal   80x80x16 to 40x40x128, 5x5 stride 2    128 of 128 channels
  cal_k3       the same at 3x3                        128 of 128 channels
  cal_oc16     the same at 16 output channels          16 of 16 channels
  mn_conv0     224x224x3, declined                     correct on the CPU
  mobilenet    declined throughout                     correct on the CPU

The encoder was also checked against a register stream captured from a working
driver on the same board, 143 of 143 entries identical with the order included.

Two things worth a maintainer's attention

The SoC is detected by scanning the platform bus for a device whose compatible
names an rknn core, because the accel node cannot lead there. Its parent is the
synthetic platform device the driver creates to aggregate the cores, and the
cores that carry the OF nodes are siblings of it rather than ancestors. This
wants to be a parameter on the rocket uAPI instead, and I am happy to write that
if it is welcome. If no core names a SoC the screen refuses to open rather than
guessing, because guessing encodes an RK3576 as an RK3588 and the hardware then
reads addresses that are not addresses.

DPU 0x4050 is emitted per output channel count on RK3576, where upstream emits a
constant. Igor Paunovic decoded the two values against registers.xml and they
differ only in SIZE_E_2. He measured RK3588 at 56, 88 and 120 output channels
against an unclamped per channel reference and found no toggle is needed there,
while on RK3576 forcing the constant times out at 16 output channels and gives 0
of 41 at 41. Each form fails where the other works, so the field is real on this
SoC. That exchange is on the linux-media thread for v7 of the kernel side.

The development history this was distilled from is at
https://github.com/gahingwoo/mesa-rk3576 if the reasoning behind a value is
ever wanted. It is not proposed for upstream.
