Title: rocket: RK3576 NPU enablement, regular convolution

---

The minimal series to get one convolution running on the RK3576 NPU. Five
patches, 703 lines, no debug environment variables.

It covers a regular convolution over uint8 per tensor operands, one task per
operation. RK3576 does not use the RK3588 register map at the same offsets, so
it gets its own encoder, and its per channel requantisation lives in 64 byte
records rather than a flat array of biases. Depthwise, pointwise and up to four
channel image convolutions want other datapaths and weight layouts, so
rkt_ml_operation_supported declines them and they run on the CPU.

Verified on a Radxa ROCK 4D, every output channel within one count of the CPU
reference

  conv2d-cal   80x80x16 to 40x40x128, 5x5 stride 2    128 of 128 channels
  cal_k3       the same at 3x3                        128 of 128 channels
  cal_oc16     the same at 16 output channels          16 of 16 channels
  mn_conv0     224x224x3, declined                     correct on the CPU
  mobilenet    declined throughout                     correct on the CPU

Against exact integer arithmetic rather than the tflite interpreter, those two
convolutions are 99.80 and 99.79 percent of pixels identical, and the
interpreter is 0.03 and 0.05 percent away from exact on the same surfaces. Its
requant rounds a half away from zero, so the two disagree wherever the
multiplier makes the final divide a small power of two.

The encoder matches a register stream captured from a working driver on the
same board, 139 of 139 configuration entries including the order, plus four
operation enables this series adds at the end.

Patch three lifts the accumulator addend, without which every output is pinned
at or above the output zero point: the output stage applies its floor before
its offset, so the two move together. The commit message has the arithmetic.

Two things worth a maintainer's attention

The SoC is found by scanning the platform bus for a compatible naming an rknn
core, because the accel node's parent is the synthetic device aggregating the
cores and the cores carrying the OF nodes are its siblings. A parameter on the
rocket uAPI would be better and I am happy to write it. Without a match the
screen refuses to open rather than guess.

DPU 0x4050 is emitted per output channel count on RK3576 where upstream emits a
constant. The rule is the parity of DIV_ROUND_UP(oc, 16), measured at 88 and
120 where it disagrees with the oc modulo 32 rule it replaced: parity gives 88
of 88 and 120 of 120, modulo gives 64 and 96 and hangs the block at both. Igor
Paunovic measured RK3588 at 56, 88 and 120 and found no toggle is needed there.

The history this was distilled from, including the reasoning behind values that
look arbitrary, is at https://github.com/gahingwoo/mesa-rk3576. It is not
proposed for upstream.
