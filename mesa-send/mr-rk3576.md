Title: rocket: RK3576 NPU enablement, regular convolution

---

The minimal series to get one convolution running on the RK3576 NPU. Five
patches, 703 lines, no debug environment variables.

It covers a regular convolution over uint8 per tensor operands, one task per
operation. RK3576 does not use the RK3588 register map at the same offsets, so
it gets its own encoder, and its per channel requantisation lives in 64 byte
records rather than a flat array of biases. Depthwise, pointwise and up to four
channel image convolutions want other datapaths and weight layouts and are not
implemented, so rkt_ml_operation_supported declines them and they run on the
CPU.

Verified on a Radxa ROCK 4D against the CPU reference, every output channel
within one count

  conv2d-cal   80x80x16 to 40x40x128, 5x5 stride 2    128 of 128 channels
  cal_k3       the same at 3x3                        128 of 128 channels
  cal_oc16     the same at 16 output channels          16 of 16 channels
  mn_conv0     224x224x3, declined                     correct on the CPU
  mobilenet    declined throughout                     correct on the CPU

The encoder also matches a register stream captured from a working driver on
the same board, 139 of 139 configuration entries including the order, this
series adding four operation enables at the end that the captured stream has no
counterpart for, since the working stack patches those in at submit time.

An earlier version of this description said the three convolution rows were
scored against max(cpu, output zero point), that the driver had a clamp at the
output zero point I had not located, and that it was not the silicon. The first
two were true and the third was wrong. The output stage is

  byte = clamp(max(requant + L, 0) + offset, -128, 127) + 128

with the floor applied before the offset, so a negative requant is gone before
the offset can do anything about it. The operation's own
clamp(requant + out_zp, 0, 255) needs L + offset to equal out_zp - 128, and
needs L to be at least 128 so the floor never bites. This driver shipped 0 with
an offset of out_zp - 0x80, which satisfies the sum and lets the floor pin
every output at or above the output zero point. 128 with the offset 128 lower
is the pair that does not, and it is in the third patch. The rows above are
against the raw CPU output.

Two things worth a maintainer's attention

The SoC is found by scanning the platform bus for a compatible that names an
rknn core, because the accel node's parent is the synthetic device that
aggregates the cores and the cores carrying the OF nodes are siblings of it. A
parameter on the rocket uAPI would be better and I am happy to write it. Without
a match the screen refuses to open rather than guess.

DPU 0x4050 is emitted per output channel count on RK3576 where upstream emits a
constant, the two values differing only in SIZE_E_2. Forcing the constant times
out at 16 output channels and gives 0 of 41 at 41. Igor Paunovic measured RK3588
at 56, 88 and 120 and found no toggle is needed there. That exchange is on the
v7 kernel thread, on dri-devel and linux-rockchip.

The history this was distilled from is at
https://github.com/gahingwoo/mesa-rk3576 if the reasoning behind a value is ever
wanted. It is not proposed for upstream.
