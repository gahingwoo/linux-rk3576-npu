round 403, 2026-09-13 — "the hardware is the wall" was a division somebody did
                        in a report, and it was wrong

r401 and r402 both end with the same sentence: the fp16 attention matmuls run
at 0.119 TMAC/s against the int4 path's 0.45 to 0.70, both are skinny on one
axis, and that is structural. That number was arithmetic done on a stage table
in a write-up. Nothing measured it, and it is wrong by a factor of four in the
direction that flatters the claim.

  board     ROCK 4D, boot 2606ab9f, npu 594 MHz, cpu 2016/2208 MHz pinned
  binaries  /root/charsiu_run_mac 27dea2e, /root/npu_fp16_test (new target)
  model     Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct-Q4_0.gguf, 852 token prompt, -c 1024

=====================================================================
1. THE RATE, READ RATHER THAN DERIVED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
`f->macs` sums m*k*n over every op submitted. In one run, same process:

    scores   13.0 GMAC in 507 ms of fence   0.026 TMAC/s   2881 us a submit
    values   18.2 GMAC in 381 ms of fence   0.048 TMAC/s   2167 us a submit
    int4 n=2048   343.1 GMAC in 518 ms      0.66 TMAC/s
    int4 n=8192   457.4 GMAC in 947 ms      0.48 TMAC/s
    int4 n=512     28.6 GMAC in  80 ms      0.36 TMAC/s

1) NOT 0.119. Fourteen to twenty-five times below the int4 path, not six.

=====================================================================
2. IS IT DISPATCH? NO, AND THE ARM ONLY EVER MAKES IT WORSE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
2881 us for a 32 task submit is 90 us a task, and a task is 2.3 MMAC -- about
4.6 us of arithmetic at the rate the same silicon manages on int4. If that 90
us were per-task overhead, then halving the attention row block should roughly
DOUBLE the fence at constant total arithmetic. CHARSIU_ATTN_NPU_MMAX exists to
ask; it only ever makes the blocks smaller, so the answer cannot come from a
bigger allocation somewhere.

    tasks      x1     x2     x4     x8
    attn     2.77   3.05   3.60   4.67 ms a row

2) A STRAIGHT LINE, 2.50 + 0.27 * T. So a task costs 20 us, not 90, and at
the shipped block size dispatch is 0.27 of 2.77 -- TEN PERCENT of attention.

3) WHICH KILLS THE MERGE. Four query heads share a KV surface under GQA, so
32 ops could be 8. That is a 4x cut in task count and by this line it is worth
0.20 ms a row, SEVEN PERCENT. I had it in my head as 3x before this arm
existed, from the same 90 us that section 1 shows is not overhead.

4) AND IT IS NOT CORE COUNT. CHARSIU_FP16_JOBS=split sends N jobs of one
task instead of one job of N chained tasks, so the scheduler may use both NPU
cores. 2.91 against 2.76 ms a row, twice each: SLOWER. Text identical either
way, so the two-core corruption is not what is being avoided here -- the
second core simply does not help.

=====================================================================
3. IT IS THE SHAPE, AND THAT IS MEASURABLE INSIDE fp16
---------------------------------------------------------------------
npu_fp16_test prices a matmul at a chosen k and n. Same dtype, same m = 78,
same group, only the shape moving:

       k      n    MMAC a round   fence ms    TMAC/s
      64    864         34.5        1.322      0.026    <- the scores shape
    1024     64         40.9        1.269      0.032    <- the values shape
    1024   1024        654          3.453      0.190

5) SIXTEEN TIMES THE ARITHMETIC FOR 2.6x THE FENCE. Six to seven of the
twenty times attention gives away is the SHAPE, inside fp16, with the dtype
and the row count and the group size all held still.

6) AND THE SHAPE CANNOT BE CHANGED BY BATCHING. k is the head_dim for the
scores matmul and n is the head_dim for the values one. The heads that share a
surface share the WEIGHT, so merging them stacks ROWS -- it grows m, and
section 2 is the measurement that says m is already nearly free. Merging
across kv heads would fatten n at the cost of computing eight times the
output and using an eighth of it.

=====================================================================
4. WHAT IS ACTUALLY LEFT, AND WHAT IT WOULD BE WORTH
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The twenty times splits into about six to seven of shape, measured above, and
the rest between fp16 and int4 -- 0.190 at k=1024 n=1024 against 0.36 to 0.69
for int4, which is two to three and a half times but at shapes that are not
the same, so it is a bound and not a number.

7) ATTENTION'S WEIGHTS ARE THE KV CACHE AND WE PACK THEM OURSELVES. That is
the whole of the remaining road: charsiu_fp16_w_alloc builds those surfaces,
so they could be int4 or int8 and the activation could stay fp16 -- which is
exactly the w4a16 arrangement the projections already use at 0.36 to 0.69.
Nothing about attention requires fp16 weights; it is where this path started.

If it bought the dtype factor and nothing else, attention's 2343 ms at 852
tokens would be about 780, TTFT would be about 5630, and the vendor's 6027 at
885 of their tokens would be BEHIND for the first time. That is an arithmetic
projection from a bounded factor and not a result.

8) THE FIRST MEASUREMENT OF THAT ROAD DOES NOT EXIST YET: int4 at the
attention shapes. charsiu_int4 takes m, k and n and prints no time;
charsiu_matmul the same; npu_fp16_test is fp16 only. The bound in section 4 is
two shapes apart and that is the gap to close before any of this is built.

9) THE SHAPE OF MY OWN ERROR. "The hardware is the wall" was written from a
stage table, put in two board logs, the README and a handoff, and read back to
the user as a conclusion. The three instruments that falsify it -- a MAC
counter, a knob that makes the blocks smaller, and a static build of a probe
that already existed -- took under an hour between them. A number that closes
a road should be the most measured number in the round, not the least.
