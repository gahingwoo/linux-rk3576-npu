round 420, 2026-09-24 -- is v13 09/14's settle delay the domain or the rail?

Heiko Stuebner asked on v13 09/14 whether the async SError the settle delay
prevents is the power domain at all, or the NPU rail needing longer to come
up. He had seen the rail version of it on RK3588 (8acfb165a492) and asked for
the regulator to be made always-on and the SError reproduced. Chaoyi Chen
answered from Rockchip the same day: the delay is the domain's own design,
about 15 us of internal preparation during which the QoS registers must not
be touched.

This round asks the question on the configuration v13 actually posts. The
SError that justified 09/14 was observed in June under a different one: the
rail held up by a local regulator-always-on hack, no need_regulator, no
domain-supply. Nobody had removed the delay since the rail began following
the domain.

WHAT IT SAYS

1) 09/14 IS needed on what v13 posts. Take the 15 us out and the first cold
   power-on faults. This was the question worth asking before Ulf Hansson
   picks the patch up, and it could have gone the other way.

2) It is not the rail. Both settle-0 arms faulted at ~1.44 s, the first
   power-on after boot, when vdd_npu_s0 had been up since the PMIC came out
   of reset: it is regulator-boot-on at 750 mV, and the regulator core finds
   it enabled, so need_regulator's first regulator_enable() is not a ramp.
   The always-on arm removes even the possibility. The fault needs only the
   domain, and 15 us of settle removes it. That is what Chaoyi said.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SAY

- n is one boot per settle-0 arm. Both died at the same point with the same
  PC, and the control survived 536 power-ons, but two boots are two boots.
- Nothing about later power-ons with and without rail cycling: neither
  settle-0 arm lived past the first one, so the rail-cycling half of Heiko's
  hypothesis was never exercised. It did not need to be for this fault.
- Nothing about a delay shorter than 15 us. 15 is the vendor table's value
  and the one Chaoyi gives; 0 fails. The margin between them was not swept.
