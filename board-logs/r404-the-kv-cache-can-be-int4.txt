round 404, 2026-09-13 — the missing measurement: what the KV cache's dtype is
                        worth, and how far it gets

r403 decomposed charsiu's attention deficit into shape (6 to 7 times, measured
inside fp16) and dtype (2 to 3.5 times, BOUNDED and not measured, because the
two sides came from probes at different shapes). It named the gap: no tool
here priced a matmul of the caller's chosen shape in anything but fp16.
charsiu_int4 --cost is that tool.

  board     ROCK 4D, boot 2606ab9f, npu 594 MHz, cpu 2016/2208 MHz pinned
  binary    /root/charsiu_int4_cost  c85421c
  --cost IS A TIMING PROBE AND CHECKS NOTHING. The weight buffer holds what
    the setup left in it and the output is never read. The MAC array does not
    branch on data, so a dispatch of the right shape and dtype costs what it
    costs -- but every number below is a COST and never an answer.

=====================================================================
1. ALL THREE WEIGHT DTYPES, ONE PROBE, ONE BATCHING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
m = 78, fp16 activation throughout, one job a submit:

    shape                fp16 w    int8 w    int4 w      TMAC/s
    k=64   n=864          0.024     0.058     0.050    <- the scores matmul
    k=1024 n=64           0.049     0.071     0.087    <- the values matmul
    k=1024 n=1024         0.203     0.351     0.228

1) 2.4x ON THE SCORES SHAPE AND 1.8x ON THE VALUES ONE. r403's bound was 2
to 3.5 and it is inside it, which is the first thing a bound should do.

2) AND THE TWO MATMULS WANT DIFFERENT DTYPES. int8 wins at k = 64, where
int4's unpacking has only four atoms to amortise over; int4 wins at k = 1024,
where the bandwidth saving is what matters. A design that picked one for both
would leave a quarter of this on the table.

3) THESE ARE A LOWER BOUND FOR THE RUNTIME. Every row carries a full
per-submit cost -- 59 to 183 us of it -- and the real path puts 32 jobs in one
submit. That cost is dtype-independent, so amortising it WIDENS these ratios.

=====================================================================
2. WHAT IT WOULD BE WORTH, AND WHY THAT IS NOT ENOUGH ON ITS OWN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
At 852 tokens the layer is 1996 ms: fence 893, softmax 358, and 745 of pack,
cache syncs, plan and readback.

    fence, scores   507 / 2.4  ->  211 ms
    fence, values   386 / 1.8  ->  214 ms
    the layer       1996       -> 1528 ms      attention 2.75 -> 2.11 ms a row
    TTFT at 852     7191       -> 6646 ms

    the vendor, same text, 885 of their tokens          6027 ms
    their lead      1.193x     -> about 1.10x

4) SO THE KV DTYPE ALONE DOES NOT BEAT THEM. It closes about half of what
is left. The arithmetic above is a projection from a measured factor onto a
measured stage table, which is a better class of guess than the one r401 made,
and it is still a guess.

5) WHAT WOULD HAVE TO COME WITH IT, all of it measured and none of it
hardware: the cache syncs are 301 ms (psync 135, plan 166) and they are
charged on WHOLE buffer objects that int4 surfaces make a quarter the size;
the softmax is 358 and still makes three passes a row; the pack is 200. Half
of that 860 is about 430 ms, which would put TTFT near 6200 against their
6027 -- level, not ahead.

6) AND ONE MORE THING IS SITTING IN PLAIN SIGHT. The values matmul
contracts over kv, the V surface's allocated extent, which climbs by DOUBLING:
at 852 tokens kv is 1024 while the average live position count is 426. **58%
of that matmul multiplies by zeros.** The surface cannot be run at a smaller k
than it was packed at -- that is the layout law in charsiu.h -- but the ladder
it climbs is a choice, and a linear one wastes less AND repacks fewer times
above 256. It buys nothing at 852 (1024 either way) and up to 25% in the bands
between powers of two.

=====================================================================
3. THE SHAPE OF THE DAY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
7) r401 said the arm could not win at any length; it wins above 376 tokens.
r402 said the hardware was the wall; it is 10% dispatch, 6 to 7 times shape,
and a dtype factor that is now measured at 1.8 to 2.4 and actionable. Both
were written from arithmetic done on a stage table rather than from an
instrument, and both took under an hour to falsify once an instrument existed.

The thing worth keeping is not either number. It is that three times in one
day the sentence that closed a road was the least measured sentence in the
round.
