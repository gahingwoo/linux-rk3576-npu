r426: the settle delay belongs on NPUTOP, not on NPU0/NPU1
===========================================================

2026-11-12, Radxa ROCK 4D, Armbian 26.11.0-trunk (Ubuntu 24.04 desktop), the
same card, dtb and protocol as r425 (dtb A, md5 1201a529).

Why: Finley Xiao (Rockchip) replied to v16 9/16 on 2026-11-11 that the 15 us
settle is needed on PD_NPUTOP only, not on PD_NPU0/PD_NPU1, because genpd
powers nputop first and the time has elapsed by the time a core domain
switches on; and that PD_VOP needs 15 us as well. The vendor kernel
(6.1.115) has exactly that table: nputop 15, npu0 0, npu1 0, vop 15. v14 to
v16, and v15 9/15 as applied to ulfh/linux-pm.git next (d97a124b7153), put
15 on all three NPU domains and 0 on vop.

Kernel: Armbian main 2d3dd98f plus one patch after rk3576-npu-v14
(rk3576-npu-zz-delay-test.patch, applied as 274/364 per the build log): npu0
and npu1 15 -> 0, vop 0 -> 15. Built on the fork's CI, run 43164250530
(gahingwoo/build branch rk3576-npu-delay-test, d5d3c07f), installed as the
linux-image deb only. Not a clean single-variable change: the CI's compiler
is Ubuntu gcc 13.3, the official package's is Debian gcc 14.2. rocket.ko md5
e4c26b8c (official d91ac64f). The official kernel is kept in
/root/kbak/kernel-7.3.4-official.tgz on the card.

  [D1]     warm reboot
  [D2cold] the board unplugged and plugged back in; the previous boot's
           journal ends without a shutdown

  [D2cold] dtb=1201a529 boot=0a0a0385 runs=30 fail=0 npu0_off_before=29/30 hashes= 30 5e3aaf1a5a57; timeouts=0 iommu=0 serror=0
  [D1] dtb=1201a529 boot=6543dca0 runs=30 fail=0 npu0_off_before=30/30 hashes= 30 5e3aaf1a5a57; timeouts=0 iommu=0 serror=0

Both arms: every run completed, all 60 outputs have the text of every r425
arm (5e3aaf1a5a57), no job timeout, no IOMMU error, no SError, and the boot
journal has no SError, no "failed to set idle" and no chain status error.
In D2 one run of 30 started with npu0 not yet off, so 29 of its 30 were full
power-ups of the core domain.

What this shows: with the delay on nputop only, the NPU core domains power
up cleanly from a warm reboot and from a power-off, 30 times each, on one
board, which is what Finley says and what the vendor table does.

What it does not show: anything about PD_VOP. Adding 15 us there changed
nothing visible, but no failure without it was ever seen here, so whether
VOP needs it rests on Rockchip's word, not on this board.
