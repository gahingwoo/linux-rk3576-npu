round 405, 2026-09-13 — the number the whole plan was aimed at was never
                        determined, and the vendor's own file says where to
                        look instead

"The quadratic term is the whole deficit and theirs is six times smaller" has
organised this work since r393. It came from least squares `a + bn + cn^2` on
eight points a side: charsiu 0.004483, vendor 0.000743, ratio 6.0. r402 already
showed the fit is not the instrument for COMPARING two curves. This round asks
whether it determines its own c at all.

=====================================================================
1. ⛔ IT DOES NOT, AND TWO INDEPENDENT READINGS SAY SO
---------------------------------------------------------------------
**The same configuration, two boots.** r402 re-ran the CPU-attention ladder --
the same configuration r393 fitted -- in one boot with a drift control:

    r393    187.9 / 5.456 / 0.004483
    r402    196.4 / 5.278 / 0.004974      an 11% swing in c

from curves that agree to 3% point by point at seven of eight lengths. Over
eight points the three parameters trade against each other; a lower c is
bought with a higher b.

**The marginal, which does not trade them.** (T(n2)-T(n1))/(n2-n1) is b + 2cn,
so the slope of the marginal column IS 2c, and it is local rather than global:

    from 200 tokens up    charsiu c 0.001556   vendor c 0.000974   ratio 1.60x
    from 100 tokens up    charsiu c 0.002118   vendor c 0.000111   ratio 19.1x

⛔ 1) ONE POINT MOVES IT BY TWELVE TIMES. The vendor's 135 token reading is
the worst residual in their own curve, +5.3%, and including or excluding it
takes the ratio from 1.6 to 19. Six or seven points of a noisy marginal do not
determine a slope here.

🏁 2) THE DIRECTION SURVIVES AND THE MAGNITUDE DOES NOT. Our prefill grows
faster with length than theirs: ahead at 202 tokens, behind at 852. That is a
statement about measured points and needs no fit. "Six times" is an artefact.

🔑 3) THIS IS THE 3x3 CONVOLUTION SHAPE, not the wall's. The wall was a
residual attributed to hardware. This is a RULE FITTED TO THE WRONG QUANTITY --
the same as "ic > 32" for the conv rule, which was really "ic a multiple of
16". The fit describes both curves to 2% and still does not pin either c.

=====================================================================
2. WHAT THE VENDOR'S OWN FILE SAYS, WHICH NEEDED NO FIT AT ALL
---------------------------------------------------------------------
tools/rkllm_regcmd.py reads their dispatches out of the .rkllm. 8808
convolutions, and 4940 of them carry **fp16 weights**:

    ic       oc     M    wbits   what it is
    64       32/64/96/128   32..128   16.0   the SCORES matmul  (2112 ops)
    2688..4064   64         32..48    16.0   the VALUES matmul

🏁 4) THE VENDOR RUNS ATTENTION ON THE NPU, IN fp16, AT THE SAME THIN SHAPES
WE DO. ic = 64 is the head_dim; oc = 64 with ic stepping by 32 is the values
matmul contracting over live positions. So "their attention is cheaper because
ours is the wrong shape" cannot be the explanation: it is the same shape.

🏁 5) AND OUR REGISTER STREAM MATCHES THEIRS. cmp_vendor.py at their own
values shape, M=48 K=2688 N=64 fp16/fp16: **identical 119, different 0, vendor
only 0**, charsiu only 23 -- all of them the RDMA block, which they leave set
from a previous op. A check that could have failed and did not.

⛔ 6) THEIR ic STEPS BY 32 AND OURS DOUBLES. 2688, 2720, 2752, 2784 -- the V
surface's contraction extent tracks the live positions to 32. Ours climbs by
doubling, so at 852 tokens we contract over 1024 where they would use 864.
That is 19%, measured against nothing yet, and it is the one concrete
difference the diff found.

=====================================================================
3. ⛔ AND THE SHAPE HYPOTHESIS THAT CAME OUT OF IT IS REFUTED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Their scores matmul caps oc at 128 while ours runs at npad, up to 864. The
obvious reading is that a wide oc is bad at ic = 64 and they know it.

    k=64, m=78, fp16      n     32     64    128    256    512    864
                     TMAC/s  0.005  0.010  0.015  0.020  0.024  0.026

⛔ 7) MONOTONE THE OTHER WAY. Wider is better at every step, and our n = 864
is already the best point on this curve. Their oc <= 128 is not a throughput
rule. Same for m: 0.008 / 0.013 / 0.018 at m = 32 / 64 / 128.

🔑 8) WHAT IS LEFT IS A REAL k DEPENDENCE, with the fixed cost removed:
marginal 33.5 us a MMAC at k = 64 against 4.5 at k = 1024, which is 7x and is
what a feature-atom machine does when you fill four atoms instead of sixty
four. That is the same rule the conv work landed on in a different dress.
