9/15 and the two sashiko findings on it
  U-Boot 2026.10 hands over with the NPU domains ON:
    0x27380214 = 0x00000200   (group-1 power bits: only bit 9, gpu, off)
    0x27380570 = 0x85ffffff   (repair status: bits 22..24, nputop/npu0/npu1, on)
  That is the state sashiko says makes pm_genpd_add_subdomain() fail with
  -EINVAL. It does not: every boot of this image registered all four
  domains and the NPU ran. Refuted on this board.

  Reading them again from Linux failed twice, both my doing: once on a
  powered-off domain (async SError, panic), once with the domain on but its
  clocks gated (the read hung the SoC). Both needed a power cycle.
  This log first concluded, from reading the code, that 9/15 makes the first
  NPU power-on write zeros over these values. THAT WAS WRONG, and r423
  measured it: the NPU domains have their QoS saved before their first
  power-on, and read back 0x101/3/0x80/8 after it. The domain that does get
  zeros is the GPU, on mainline without v14; see r423.
