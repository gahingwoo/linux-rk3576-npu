round 396, 2026-09-13 — the fp16 mirror holds a head per layer, and gemma-4 doubles its win

r394 found gemma-4-E2B running 7 of its 35 attention layers on the NPU and the
other 28 silently on the CPU, because its sliding-window layers are head_dim
256 where its full ones are 512 and the fp16 mirror was built for one.

⚠⚠ THAT WAS A TRADE-OFF AND NOT A BUG, and r394 was written as though it might
be one. `a->hd` is the float cache's STRIDE: attn_kcache_at and attn_vcache_at
are correct only at the stride, and the comment beside them names gemma4 --
"indexing the cache by the live head would make layer 4 read where layer 0
wrote". The mirror refused the mismatching layers rather than pack a 256 long
head into a 512 wide buffer and read 256 columns of whatever was there. With
nothing measured that was the right call. r394 and r395 measured it.

WHAT CHANGED  a->hd stays exactly what it was, the stride. A new a->lhd[layer]
              carries the layer's own head, and it is what sizes the mirror's
              buffers and what the packers are given. Eight sites; the two
              repack loops now carry BOTH widths on one line -- packed at the
              layer's head, addressed at the stride -- and say so.

              The model already knew: llama_load fills L->head_dim from
              key_length_swa for window layers and m->head_dim is already the
              LARGER of the two, because the cache is one allocation.

              The memory estimate is summed per layer now as well. Sizing every
              layer at the stride over-counted gemma-4 by a third, which is a
              cap that refuses a run the hardware would have taken.

🏁 1) 0.936 -> 0.804 AT 852 TOKENS, AND 0.804 IS THE BEST NUMBER ON THE WHOLE
AXIS -- better than gemma-3-1b's 0.877 at head_dim 256. In wall clock the
prompt goes 19900 ms to 16000, 19.6% off, where before the change it was 6.5%.
The fix is worth 14% of gemma-4's long-prompt TTFT on its own.

🏁 2) THE CROSSOVER MOVED IN, about 380 tokens to about 274.

⚠ 3) THE SHORT END GOT SLIGHTLY WORSE, 1.105 -> 1.131 at 52 tokens and 1.032 ->
1.042 at 202. 28 more layers each paying a dispatch that does not yet earn its
keep is exactly what the axis predicts below the crossover; it is not noise and
it is not a reason to stop.

⚠ 4) AND gemma-4 IS STILL NOT A CLEAN head_dim 512 POINT. It is a mixture, 7
layers at 512 and 28 at 256, and now all of them run. What it is not is a
measurement of either head_dim on its own.

⛔ 5) THE PER-LAYER ARITHMETIC IS TEMPTING AND IT DOES NOT SEPARATE THE TWO.
The 7 head_dim 512 layers are worth 6.4 points of the ratio and the 28 at 256
are worth 13.2, which is 0.91 a layer against 0.47 -- twice the benefit per
layer for twice the head. That reads like the head_dim scaling this axis was
looking for, and it is not evidence for it: the 512 layers are the GLOBAL ones
and the 256 are the sliding-window ones, so at 852 tokens a 512 layer attends
over up to 852 positions and a 256 layer over at most 512. The work ratio is
about 1.7 and the benefit ratio is 1.94. Head length and attention span move
together in this model and nothing here can pull them apart.
