round 415, 2026-09-15 - two of the nine models ship with no weight grouping at
                        all, and grouping gemma3 is worth 40% of its perplexity

npuquant falls back to one scale a row whenever k % group is non-zero, and
llama_auto_kmax() pins the group at 1024 and only ever considers widening it.
So a model whose K values are not multiples of 1024 is on the per-row path at
every width the tree will choose, and stays there. The tree knows this and
treats it as a licence to widen the slice for free; nobody asked what happens
if the group NARROWS to something those K values do divide.

  board     ROCK 4D, boot afe55e04 throughout, 594 MHz, boot entry 1,
            binary 3e3a6c40a165, performance governor.
  desk      quality, which reproduces the board to 0.005% on this measurement.
            Every ppl here is over 511 scored positions of tests/corpus/long.txt
            unless it says otherwise.

=====================================================================
1. WHICH MODELS ARE ON THE PER-ROW PATH, COUNTED RATHER THAN DERIVED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A tensor is grouped only when the group divides its K, K being the INPUT
width, and only when the group is strictly narrower than K -- a tensor that is
EXACTLY one group is refused by kgroup < k. Every K below was read off a run
with CHARSIU_NPU_VERBOSE, not worked out from n_embd and n_ff, which is how
three of these rows came out different from the guess.

    model             distinct K                       widest width that
                                                       divides all of them
    Llama-3.2-1B      2048 8192                        1024   ships grouped
    Phi-3.5-mini      3072 8192                        1024   ships grouped
    SmolLM2-1.7B      2048 8192                        1024   ships grouped
    Qwen3-0.6B        1024 2048 3072                    512
    tinyllama-1.1b    2048 5632                         512
    Qwen2.5-1.5B      1536 8960                         256
    gemma-3-1b        1024 1152 6912                    128
    gemma-4-E2B       256 1536 2048 4096 6144 12288     128

THREE OF EIGHT ARE FULLY GROUPED AT THE SHIPPING WIDTH. The other five carry
tensors on the per-row path: tinyllama's down_proj at 5632, Qwen3's 1024 wide
ones (a single group, which kgroup < k refuses), both of Qwen2.5's, gemma4's
n_embd and its 256 wide per layer projection, and all three of gemma-3-1b's.

AND THE WIDTH THAT GROUPS EVERYTHING IS NOT THE ONE TO WANT. For gemma-3-1b it
is 128, and the board charges 27% of decode for that. Section 2 is what
happens at a width that groups only the two that carry the bytes.

2. WHAT THAT COSTS gemma-3-1b: FORTY PERCENT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
    group   1152 ->   6912 ->    decode t/s        ppl     against shipping
    1024    ungrouped ungrouped   20.10        108.7063     ships
     576    2 groups  12 groups   20.70  +3.0%  79.2824     -27.1%
     384    3 groups  18 groups   18.69  -7.0%  65.4765     -39.8%
     192    6 groups  36 groups   14.68 -27.0%  57.1298     -47.4%
     128    9 groups  54 groups    -            47.0269     -56.7%
    its own q4_0, on the CPU, no charsiu requantisation:  49.0532

AND AT 128 IT PASSES THE FILE'S OWN q4_0: 47.0269 against 49.0532. llama.cpp's
q4_0 is blocks of 32 with an fp16 scale, which is finer than anything here, and
charsiu's four bits at 128 with an fp32 scale and the wsum correction still
score better on this model and this corpus. That is one model and one text and
it is not a general claim, but it is the first time anything in this tree has
been on that side of the line.

The decode column stops at 192 because that is where the board sweep stopped;
128 was measured for quality only.

Three readings an arm, medians, one boot, performance governor; the spreads
are 20.07..20.10, 20.68..20.70, 18.66..18.69 and 14.68..14.69, so the 3% is
twenty times the wider of the two arms' ranges.

576 IS NOT A TRADE. It is three percent FASTER and twenty-seven percent better,
against a shipping default that groups none of this model. 384 is the trade:
another thirteen points of quality for seven of decode. 192 is neither.

The gap to the file's own q4_0 closes from 2.22x to 1.62x at 576 and 1.33x at
384.

WHY 576 IS FASTER IS NOT SETTLED AND THE ROUND DOES NOT CLAIM IT. More groups
means more K slices, which is more read work by the tree's own formula, and
this went the other way.

The first guess was KFIT, which lets a last slice absorb its remainder instead
of being it, and only for ungrouped tensors -- which at 1024 is all of
gemma-3-1b. Reading npudev killed it before the board answered:
CHARSIU_NPU_KFIT defaults to 0, so no slice absorbs anything and the geometry
at 1024 is 1152 -> 1024 + 128 and 6912 -> six of 1024 plus 768. At 576 it is
two of 576 and twelve of 576.

So the slice COUNT goes 2 -> 2 and 7 -> 12 and the decode goes UP. What is
also true is that the 1024 geometry has a 128 wide slice in it, an eighth of a
slot, and a slice costs a dispatch and a full read of n outputs whatever it
carries. Balance rather than count is the candidate now, and it is a guess
until something measures it.

KFIT itself has never been priced -- npudev says "Off until a board round says
it is both correct and faster" -- and this round put both arms on the board
while it was there, which is in section 7.

THE UNGROUPED ROW IS A CONTROL AND IT HAD TO BE RUN. A group width of 99991

Three readings an arm, medians, one boot. 512 is twelve percent of quality for

side of. 256 is not: 28% of decode, and the quality half was not measured
because a trade that has already lost on one axis does not need the other.

=====================================================================
4. NO SINGLE WIDTH CAN GROUP A WHOLE MODEL, AND THAT IS THE REAL LIMIT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
gemma-3-1b's three K values are 1152, 6912 and 1024. 576 and 384 divide the
first two; neither divides 1024, so its o_proj stays on the per-row path at
every width that helps the rest. There is no width that groups all three.

tensor_grouped() requires kgroup == kmax, so the group is one number for the
whole model and this cannot be worked around from the outside. The reason for
that condition is real: add_slice reads a slice's scale at k0 / kgroup, so a
slice spanning two groups would apply the first group's scale to both.

So the lever is a PER TENSOR group width, which needs the scale gather to carry
more than one group per slice. That is an implementation limit and not a
hardware one, and section 2 is what it would be worth.

AND KMAX IS NOT PART OF IT. The two knobs move together only because of that
condition; on the host they separate, and

    gemma4  KMAX 1024  GROUP 512   112.4869
    gemma4  KMAX  512  GROUP 512   112.4869

are identical to the digit. The slice width contributes nothing to the
arithmetic. A wide slice with narrow groups is the thing worth building.

5. THE HOUR I SPENT BELIEVING THE SHIPPING RUNTIME HAD STOPPED GROUPING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The board regression came back with Llama at ppl 41.2777. paper-evidence
records 33.8071 for the grouped arm and 41.5289 for the ungrouped one. 41.2777
is four tenths of a percent from the ungrouped figure.

It is not that figure. Every number in that table is -n 300, which scores 299
positions; charsiu_ppl with no -n scores 511. The pack's own command run
verbatim reproduces 33.8071 to the digit, and the true ungrouped number at 511
is 50.7838, nowhere near.

THE TELL WAS ON SCREEN THE WHOLE TIME: the tool prints "over 299 scored
positions of 300 tokens" against "over 511 scored positions of 512" on every
line. Two numbers landing four tenths of a percent apart do not announce that
they are different measurements. A perplexity belongs to the model file, the
corpus file AND the length; the pack says so now.

AND BEFORE THAT THE INSTRUMENT WAS DEAD. The first three quality arms returned
the same number to four decimals at 1024, 512 and 256. An arm equal to its
control to the last digit means it never ran: CHARSIU_NPU_W4V=1 was missing, so
the quantiser was at eight bits, where the group width does not participate. A
control on Llama, where the widths certainly should differ, is what caught it.

=====================================================================
6. gemma4 IS NOT MONOTONE AND THE REASON IS NOT WHAT I GUESSED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
    gemma4   group 1024 (1536 ungrouped)   89.0370
             group  512 (3 groups)        112.4869   WORSE than ungrouped
             group  256 (6 groups)         76.8166
             its own q4_0                  59.8873

The first hypothesis was the group COUNT: 512 is the only width where k=1536
divides into an ODD number of groups. gemma-3-1b refutes it flatly. Its whole
ladder is monotone and the odd rungs are not special:

    groups    2       3       6       9
    ppl    79.28   65.48   57.13   47.03

Three is better than two and nine is better than six, so an odd count costs
nothing there.

Not AWQ either: CHARSIU_NPU_AWQ defaults to 0.0 and no arm set it. Not the
weight cache: that is off unless CHARSIU_NPU_CACHE is set, and it was not. Both
the quantiser's group loop and npu_matvec's consumer were read and neither has
a count dependency. The second corpus is what decides whether this ordering is
a property of the weights or of one text, and that is running.

=====================================================================
7. AND NARROWING IS NOT FREE ANYWHERE ELSE, WHICH IS THE POINT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The same 1024 against 512 on the two other models of the round of record that
carry ungrouped tensors:

    model        K                  1024 t/s   512 t/s
    tinyllama    2048 5632            23.00     20.32   -11.7%
    Qwen3-0.6B   1024 2048 3072       30.68     29.00    -5.5%
    gemma-3-1b   1024 1152 6912       20.10     20.70    +3.0%   (at 576)

gemma-3-1b is the only one that gains, and it is the only one of the three
where NOTHING is grouped at 1024: tinyllama's 2048 tensors are two groups
already and Qwen3's 2048 and 3072 are two and three. So the gain is not "a
narrower group is faster"; it is something about the geometry gemma-3-1b is in
at 1024 and gets out of at 576, and the other two have nothing to get out of.

That also means the rule cannot be a constant. A narrower group is worth
between -12% and +3% of decode depending on the model's K set, and the quality
it buys is worth between nothing and -40% of perplexity on the same axis. What
this asks for is a per model decision, which is what llama_auto_kmax already
is -- it just only ever looks upward.
