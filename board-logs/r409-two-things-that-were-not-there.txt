1. ⛔ THE DEFERRED GATHER: THE FAILURE COUNT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
r408 section 7 left the deferred gather off, wrong on 5 of 9 models, cause not
found. CHARSIU_NPU_DEFER_READ became a POSITION selector so the flush could be
moved without moving anything else:

    2  at the end of the deferring call                  0 of 9 differ
    3  at the top of the next call                       5 of 9
    4  after batch_outbuf                                5 of 9
    5  after the pack and emit, before the submit        5 of 9
    1  after the submit, the only overlapping position   9 of 9
    1  repeated                                          9 of 9

That reads as a clean bisection: =2 clean says the record-and-restore machinery
is right, and the step from 5 to 1 is the pack, worth 4 of the 9. g->bd1 is in
the pack and is exactly the right shape of hazard -- its own allocation comment
says "a single array would hand every slice the last one's scales", and a
deferred gather makes that one scope up. Snapshotting it took =1 from 9 to 5.

⛔ 1) AND THEN TWO RESULTS THAT CANNOT BOTH BE TRUE OF A DETERMINISTIC FAULT:

  - the bd1 snapshot changed =3 from 5 differing to 8. At position 3 the flush
    runs before the next call has touched anything, so g->bd1 still holds the
    deferring tensor's own values and the restore writes bytes that are already
    there. A provable no-op moved the count by three models.
  - CHARSIU_DEFER_SITE=qkv defers a strict SUBSET of the sites and came back 8
    differing against the superset's 5. Fewer deferrals cannot cause more
    failures.

🔑 2) SO "N OF 9 MODELS DIFFER" IS NOT STABLE ENOUGH TO COMPARE TWO BUILDS
WITH, and every inference from 5 against 7 against 9 was reading its variance,
including the attribution of the 9-to-5 step to bd1. That attribution is
withdrawn. The snapshot stays because it is a real hazard by construction, not
because it was measured.

⚠ 3) AND I HAD CHECKED REPEATABILITY, ONCE. =1 twice on one build, 9 and 9.
That was then relied on for every later comparison, including one where the
change was a provable no-op. A control run once is not a property of the
instrument.

What repeats is the two ends: =0 is 0 of 9 twice and =2 is 0 of 9 twice on two
builds. A non-repeatable failure count points at a race or a read of memory
that is not there, rather than at a field somebody forgot to snapshot. Parked,
default off.

2. ⭐ THE REGISTER STREAM IS THE SAME BYTES EVERY LAYER BUT ONE WORD
---------------------------------------------------------------------
charsiu_emit_job depends on the shape and four addresses. A chunk's sixteen
layers ask the fp16 unit for the same shapes at the same offsets in buffers
that have not moved -- except the KV surface, which is per layer.

A cache keyed on all of it hit 0 of 176 groups, measured, which is the useful
half of that reading: it says the shapes DO repeat and names the one field
that does not.

charsiu_patch_weight_addr rewrites that word (CNA 0x1110). tests/patch_waddr.c
holds it to the only standard worth having -- the patched stream must be byte
for byte what emit_job would have produced, at every shape attention runs and
all three weight dtypes -- and the patch refuses unless exactly one word
carries the address, which is the guard against an emitter that later folds it
into a size register as well.

    171 of 176 groups on the values handle, 165 of 176 on the scores one
    emit 28 -> 10 ms and 29 -> 11

The misses are the chunk boundaries, where the prompt's extent changes n.

3. ⭐ AND THE PREP ON THE INPUT SURFACE INVALIDATES A BUFFER NOBODY WRITES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
attn_npu_layer already makes this argument for the KV surfaces: "the hardware
only ever reads these, so the CPU's copy is never stale and there is nothing to
invalidate". The fp16 input surface is the same kind of buffer. What the prep
also does is wait on the buffer's fence, and that wait is over before the next
group starts.

    psync 111 -> 62 ms on the values group, 18 -> 11 on the scores one

⚠ 4) THE PACK GIVES 13 ms OF IT BACK, because it now takes the cache misses the
invalidate used to absorb. Net about 43 ms. Reporting the psync column alone
would have claimed 56.

🏁 5) BOTH CORRECT ON ALL NINE MODELS with fp16 attention FORCED on: 9 compared,
0 differing. That arm is worth naming on its own -- the shipped threshold is
448 tokens and board_text_all's prompts are 64 to 88, so the default run has
never exercised the fp16 attention path at all. This is the first check that
does.

⚠ 6) AND ~79 ms IS BELOW WHAT THE TTFT LADDER CAN RESOLVE. The ladder's 852
point has a range of about 3% on this board, which is 200 ms. The evidence for
this round is the stage columns and the hit counters, which are direct, and
NOT a TTFT number. The shipped figure stays r408's: 7016 ms at 852 tokens
against the vendor's 6027.

⛔ 8) THE BOARD REBOOTED MID-SESSION AND CAME UP WITH debugfs NOT MOUNTED, so
every script's `$(cat /sys/kernel/debug/clk/... 2>/dev/null)` returned empty
and the headers printed "npu clk   Hz". A full TTFT ladder ran and completed
under a rate nobody could read. The rate was 594 MHz, confirmed afterwards by
mounting debugfs, but that was luck rather than evidence.

tests/board_clk.sh mounts debugfs if it has to and refuses otherwise.

⚠ 9) AND THE FIRST VERSION OF THAT REFUSAL COULD NOT FIRE: `exit 1` inside a
function used as `$(npu_clk)` ends the subshell, not the script, so it printed
a blank clock and carried on. Exactly the hole it was written to close, with a
guard in it that did nothing. Callers do `NPUCLK=$(npu_clk) || exit 1` now, and
CHARSIU_NPU_CLK_PATH exists so the refusal can be pointed at nothing and
WATCHED to fire. It does, exit status 1.

🔑 10) BOTH OF TODAY'S HOLES ARE THE SAME SENTENCE: a thing that is not there
reads exactly like a thing whose value is nothing. An unmounted directory reads
as an empty one and an unmounted debugfs reads as a clock of zero, and both
produced output that looks like a completed round.
