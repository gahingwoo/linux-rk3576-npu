The decode line is 2.8% lower and is NOT read as a regression or as nothing.
The two kernels cannot share a boot, this board drifts about 3% between
boots, section 6 does not pin the CPU governor, and each side is one
reading. Settling it needs the two entries alternated over several boots
with the governor pinned.

What this round verifies about v14 in particular: 10/15's single-reset
change runs on every NPU power-on, and the boot round plus this regression
power the domains on and off hundreds of times without a fault. The removal
path, the other half of 10/15's change, only runs when pmdomain probe fails
and is not exercised here.

OPEN, for the user: the modules. Two directories under /lib/modules belong
to kernels this board no longer boots (7.1.0-rc5 and 7.2.0-rc5, 175 MB
together). Removing them and installing only zram.ko and zsmalloc.ko, or
putting /lib/modules on the model partition once it is in fstab, would both
work. Neither was done without asking.
