round 402, 2026-09-13 — half the prefill gap against the vendor, and the
                        coefficient comparison that would have lied about it

r401 took the fp16 attention arm from losing at every length to winning above
about 376 tokens and made it the default. Attention is the whole of prefill's
quadratic term -- every other stage is linear in the prompt -- so this asks
what that did to the curve the vendor is compared against.

=====================================================================
2. ⛔ THE COEFFICIENTS SAY THE OPPOSITE OF THE CURVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Fitting a + bn + cn^2 to today's two arms:

    CPU arm     196.4 ms   5.278 ms/tok   0.004974 ms/tok^2
    default     120.2 ms   6.538 ms/tok   0.002106 ms/tok^2

The quadratic more than halves, which is the result one wants to report. Read
with the vendor's 45.8 / 6.095 / 0.000743 and their 33 template tokens folded
in, the difference is -127.5 + 0.394n + 0.001363n^2, whose crossover is about
195 tokens -- WORSE than r393's 248, from a change that made the runtime
faster at every length it touched.

⛔ 2) THE FIT IS NOT THE INSTRUMENT, AND THE CONTROL SAYS SO. The same
configuration fitted 187.9 / 5.456 / 0.004483 in r393 and 196.4 / 5.278 /
0.004974 today -- an 11% swing in the quadratic between two curves that agree
to 3% point by point. Over an eight-point ladder these three parameters are
correlated: a lower c is bought with a higher b. Comparing runtimes by their
c is comparing a number neither curve independently determines.

🔑 3) r392 KILLED "1.23x PER PROMPT TOKEN" FOR THE SAME REASON one round
earlier -- two points fitting a line -- and tools/ttft_compare.py exists
because of it. It interpolates INSIDE each curve and never fits across them.
I wrote the fit anyway and had the paragraph drafted before the control was
read.

=====================================================================
4. WHAT IS LEFT OF THE QUADRATIC
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Attention is the whole of it: every other prefill stage is linear in the
prompt. At 852 tokens ours is 2.75 ms a row, about 2340 ms. The vendor's
quadratic term over the same prompt is 0.000743 * 852^2 = 539 ms.

⛔ 7) SO THEIR ATTENTION IS STILL 4.3x CHEAPER THAN OURS, AND OUR FENCE ALONE
IS 880 ms -- more than their whole quadratic. The fp16 attention matmuls run
at 0.119 TMAC/s where the same silicon does 0.45 to 0.70 on int4, because both
of them are skinny on one axis: k = 64 for the scores, n = 64 for the values.
Closing the rest of this gap is not CPU-side work and it is not more of what
r401 did.
