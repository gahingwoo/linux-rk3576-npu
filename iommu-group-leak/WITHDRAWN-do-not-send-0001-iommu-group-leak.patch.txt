Subject: [PATCH] accel/rocket: don't leak an IOMMU group reference per retired
 job

rocket_job_handle_irq() detaches the core's IOMMU group when a job retires,
and takes a fresh reference to that group in order to name it:

	iommu_detach_group(NULL, iommu_group_get(core->dev));

iommu_detach_group() does not consume a reference. It takes the group's mutex
and calls __iommu_group_set_core_domain(), and that is all. So the reference
iommu_group_get() took is never put, and one is leaked for every job that
retires. The group's kobject then cannot reach zero, and rocket_core_fini()'s
own iommu_group_put() no longer releases it.

The group is already held for the lifetime of the core. rocket_core_init()
takes it into core->iommu_group and rocket_core_fini() puts it. The other two
call sites in this file use that member already -- the attach in
rocket_job_run() and the detach in rocket_reset() -- so this one is the odd
one out rather than a second reference somebody wanted.

Use core->iommu_group here as well.
