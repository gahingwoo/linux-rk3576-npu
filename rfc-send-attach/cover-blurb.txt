Two patches on top of "[PATCH v12 00/14] accel/rocket: RK3576 NPU (RKNN)
enablement"; the prerequisite-patch-id trailers name it. Neither applies
without it: both rework the job and reset paths that series restructures.

rocket attaches the job's IOMMU domain to the core before every job and
detaches it after, and on rk_iommu those are five poll loops and a dozen
MMIO accesses each way, to arrive at the same domain every time. It also
takes a group reference on the way out and never puts it, which Sashiko
flagged on every mail of v11. 1/2 keeps the domain attached until a job
brings a different one, the core resets, or the core goes away.

2/2 handles the job completion in the hardirq instead of waking a thread
for three register writes and a fence signal.

Measured on a Radxa ROCK 4D with an open runtime that issues about 150
jobs a core per token of a language model: decode 4% faster over four
models, text identical.

*** jobs on the two cores, that goes here with the numbers. If it says the
*** runtime was, this paragraph does not exist.

Both are untested on RK3588, which is the hardware the driver ships for.
